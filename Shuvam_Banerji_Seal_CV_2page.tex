\section{Master's Thesis (Ongoing)}

\entryv{Gray-Code and Karnaugh-Map Structure of Nucleic-Acid Sequences, Formalised in Lean 4}{2026--}{BS-MS Thesis, IISER Kolkata}{%
Encoding DNA, RNA and protein sequences into binary sequence space and arranging the codewords on Gray-code orderings and Karnaugh maps, so that map adjacency is a single-symbol mutation. Every theorem about the resulting binary relations, symmetries and distance structure is machine-checked in \textbf{Lean 4} (Mathlib).}

\entryv{A Transformer Mixture-of-Experts Model for RNA Structure Prediction}{2026--}{BS-MS Thesis, IISER Kolkata}{%
A sparse \textbf{Mixture-of-Experts transformer} for RNA secondary and tertiary structure prediction, routing structural motifs (helices, loops, pseudoknots) to specialised experts at fixed inference cost. Geometry-aware attention with distance-map and torsion-angle heads.}

\section{Flagship Open-Source Software}

\entryv{Molecule3D / LAMMPS Web GUI \href{https://github.com/Shuvam-Banerji-Seal/lammps-web-gui}{\faGithub}~\href{https://shuvam-banerji-seal.github.io/lammps-web-gui/}{\faGlobe}}{2026--}{Browser-native molecular simulation workbench; MIT, GPU-accelerated, fully client-side}{%
A complete \textbf{LAMMPS} workbench in the browser, built to lower the entry barrier to molecular dynamics. The \textbf{Script Builder} composes input decks as a drag-and-drop flowchart over a 186-command library covering the full general-command surface, locked by a coverage test against \texttt{docs.lammps.org}, and re-imports existing \texttt{in.*} scripts. The \textbf{Compiler Helper} turns 27 CMake options into ready-to-run build scripts. The \textbf{Structure Viewer} renders LAMMPS data, XYZ, PDB and CIF plus \texttt{dump} trajectories, with playback, MP4 capture, measurement tools and PBC-aware RDF, MSD and density analysis. \textbf{Stack:} TypeScript, React, Three.js / R3F, WebGL, Vitest (143 tests).}
