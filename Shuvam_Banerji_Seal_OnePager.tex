	\section{Research Focus}

	 {\tiny
	  \textbf{Master's Thesis I -- Genomic Sequences \& Formal Mathematics:} Encoding DNA, RNA and protein sequences into binary sequence space on \textbf{Gray-code} orderings and \textbf{Karnaugh maps}, so map adjacency is a single-symbol mutation; every theorem machine-checked in \textbf{Lean 4} (Mathlib).\\[2pt]
	  \textbf{Master's Thesis II -- RNA Structure Prediction:} Sparse \textbf{Mixture-of-Experts transformer} for RNA secondary and tertiary structure, routing helices, loops and pseudoknots to specialised experts; geometry-aware attention with distance-map and torsion-angle heads.\\[2pt]
	  \textbf{Computational Chemistry:} Density functional theory with \textbf{Quantum ESPRESSO} and Gaussian; molecular dynamics in LAMMPS; ML surrogates for simulation.\\[2pt]
	  \textbf{Core AI/ML \& Agentic Systems:} Retrieval-augmented generation, \textbf{AI safety} and sycophancy evaluation, agentic AI, information theory.
